This installs NITEDRIVE, a polyphonic synthesiser plugin, into the system-wide plug-in folders that Ableton Live and other hosts scan:

  • /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components

You can pick which formats to install on the next screen.

If you previously copied NITEDRIVE into your own ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins folder, the installer will move that older copy aside rather than leave two versions on the scan path. It is renamed, not deleted, so you can put it back.
