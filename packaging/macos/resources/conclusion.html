NITEDRIVE is installed.

In Ableton Live, open Preferences → Plug-Ins and press Rescan. NITEDRIVE then appears under Plug-Ins as an instrument.

If the Audio Unit does not show up, Live is holding a cached scan. Quit Live, run killall -9 AudioComponentRegistrar in Terminal, and reopen it.

Start with the Nite Bass and Rave Stab presets — they are in the plugin's own preset menu, and also appear as programs in the host.
